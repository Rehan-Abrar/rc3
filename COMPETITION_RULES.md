## Format overview

- Participants: individual participants or teams (**maximum two members per team**). Participants must bring their own laptops.
- Task: **two rounds** of tasks related to Web development.
- Total working time: **five hours** to complete the tasks.
- **Use of AI models is allowed.**
- Judging is based on functionality, creativity and design.
- Scheduled breaks are incorporated into the day.

## Detailed schedule

| Time | Activity | Details |

Working windows: **Round 1 = 9:30–12:00 (2.5 h)** and **Round 2 = 1:00–3:30 (2.5 h)** → five hours total. Hard stop on development is **3:30 PM**; 3:30–5:00 is judging only.

## Competition rules

### Task instructions

- Teams receive a detailed **problem statement at the start** of the competition.
- Solutions must address **all specified requirements**, including functionality, aesthetics and responsiveness.
- **Any technology / programming stack** may be used.
- Each solution will be **evaluated against test cases**.
- The decision of the judges is **final**.

### Break management

- A single refreshment break is provided after the creation phase (12:00–1:00).

### Submission guidelines

- Submissions must include **all necessary files** (source code, assets).
- **On-spot evaluations** of the submissions.
- Deliverables may be submitted through the **designated platform or via USB**.

### Evaluation criteria (judging weights)

| Criterion | Weight | Question the judges ask |
|---|---|---|
| **Functionality** | **40%** | Does the solution meet all technical requirements? |
| **Design** | **30%** | Is the design user-friendly, visually appealing and innovative? |
| **Creativity** | **20%** | Does the solution show originality? |
| **Presentation** | **10%** | How well is the solution explained to the judges? |

Functionality + design = 70% of the score; both must be strong.

## Additional notes

- **Task supervision:** supervisors provide guidance during the task but will **not** assist in problem-solving.
- **Break management:** participants are encouraged to relax and recharge.
- **Technical support:** a dedicated team assists with hardware or software issues.
- **Decision of the judges is final.**
